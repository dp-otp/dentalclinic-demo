Cosmetic Dentistry

Transform Your Smile

Our cosmetic treatments can help you achieve the smile you've always wanted. From whitening to veneers, we offer affordable private options to enhance your natural beauty.

Teeth Whitening

Professional brightening

Professional teeth whitening delivers dramatically brighter results than over-the-counter products. We offer both in-office treatments and take-home kits for your convenience.

  • Removes years of staining from coffee, tea, and wine
  • Safe, supervised treatment by dental professionals
  • Results up to 8 shades whiter
  • Take-home kits available for maintenance

Porcelain Veneers & Inlays

Natural-looking restorations

Porcelain veneers are thin shells bonded to the front of teeth to correct chips, gaps, discolouration, or misalignment. Inlays provide durable, aesthetic restorations for damaged teeth.

  • Custom-made to match your natural teeth
  • Stain-resistant porcelain material
  • Long-lasting results (10-15 years)
  • Minimal tooth preparation required
Book Consultation

White Fillings

Mercury-free restorations

Replace old metal amalgam fillings with tooth-coloured composite resin that blends seamlessly with your natural teeth. A healthier, more aesthetic choice.

  • Mercury-free, biocompatible material
  • Matches your natural tooth colour
  • Bonds directly to tooth structure
  • Can replace existing amalgam fillings
Book Consultation

Sports Mouth Guards

Custom protection

Protect your smile during sports with custom-fitted mouth guards. Far superior to shop-bought options, our guards offer optimal protection and comfort.

  • Custom-moulded to your teeth
  • Superior fit and comfort
  • Available in various colours
  • Suitable for all contact sports

Restorative Dentistry

Restore Your Smile

We repair and replace damaged or missing teeth using modern techniques and materials, restoring both function and aesthetics to your smile.

Broken Teeth Repair

Emergency & planned care

Chipped, cracked, or broken teeth can be expertly repaired using bonding, veneers, or crowns depending on the extent of damage. Don't suffer in silence.

  • Same-day emergency appointments available
  • Multiple treatment options
  • Natural-looking results
  • Pain-free treatment with anaesthesia

Dental Crowns

Protect & strengthen

Crowns cap damaged or weakened teeth, restoring their strength, shape, and appearance. We use porcelain for the most natural-looking results.

  • Protects weak or damaged teeth
  • Restores full chewing function
  • Matches your natural tooth colour
  • Long-lasting durability (15+ years)
Book Consultation

Dental Bridges

Fill the gaps

Bridges replace one or more missing teeth by anchoring artificial teeth to neighbouring natural teeth. A fixed, comfortable solution.

  • Fixed in place - no removal needed
  • Restores your smile and bite
  • Prevents teeth from shifting
  • Natural appearance and feel

Dentures

Full & partial options

Modern dentures look incredibly natural and fit comfortably. We offer full and partial dentures to replace multiple missing teeth.

  • Custom-made for perfect fit
  • Modern, natural-looking teeth
  • Improves eating and speaking
  • Easy to maintain and clean

Protect Your Oral Health

Prevention is better than cure. Our preventative services help you maintain healthy teeth and gums, catching problems early before they become serious.

Children's Dentistry

Root Canal Treatment

Save your natural tooth

  • Usually completed in 1-2 visits
