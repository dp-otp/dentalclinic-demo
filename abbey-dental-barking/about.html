Our Story

From Abbey Road to Arboretum Place

Abbey Dental Practice has been a cornerstone of dental care in Barking for over two decades. Originally located on Abbey Road, our practice has grown alongside the community we serve.

We recently moved to our brand new, purpose-built facility at 2 Arboretum Place. This modern practice has been designed with patient comfort and clinical excellence in mind, featuring state-of-the-art equipment and a welcoming environment.

"Our mission is to provide patient-focused, quality dental service in a safe and friendly environment. We believe everyone deserves access to excellent dental care, which is why we welcome both NHS and private patients."

Our experienced team is committed to continuing the tradition of compassionate, comprehensive dental care that has made Abbey Dental Practice a trusted name in Barking.
