Facial Aesthetics

Enhance your appearance with professional aesthetic treatments. Our trained clinicians offer safe, effective anti-wrinkle treatments using their expert knowledge of facial anatomy.

Professional Anti-Wrinkle Treatments

Facial aesthetics treatments have become increasingly popular as a way to reduce the signs of ageing and achieve a more refreshed appearance. At Abbey Dental, our trained clinicians offer professional botulinum toxin (Botox) treatments in a safe, clinical environment.

Before & After Photo

Why Choose a Dentist for Facial Aesthetics?

Dentists are ideally qualified to perform facial aesthetic treatments. With extensive training in facial anatomy, injection techniques, and patient care, our clinicians can deliver safe, precise, and natural-looking results.

  • Expert knowledge of facial muscles and anatomy
  • Years of experience with injection techniques
  • Sterile clinical environment
  • Full medical history assessment
  • Follow-up care and adjustments if needed

What to Expect

Your treatment begins with a consultation where we discuss your concerns and goals. The actual treatment takes just 10-15 minutes:

  • Small amounts of botulinum toxin are injected into targeted muscles
  • Minimal discomfort - most patients describe it as a tiny pinch
  • No downtime - return to normal activities immediately
  • Results appear within 3-7 days and last 3-4 months
  • Touch-up appointments available if needed
Treatment Photo

Is It Safe?

Botulinum toxin has been used safely in medicine for over 30 years. When administered by a trained professional, it has an excellent safety profile. Side effects are typically mild and temporary, such as slight bruising at the injection site.

During your consultation, we'll review your medical history and ensure this treatment is appropriate for you. We believe in natural-looking results that enhance your appearance while maintaining facial expressiveness.

Investment

Facial aesthetic treatments are private treatments only (not available on the NHS). Pricing depends on the areas treated. Contact us for a consultation to discuss your needs and receive a personalised quote.
