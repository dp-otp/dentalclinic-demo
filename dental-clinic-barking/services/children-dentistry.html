Children's Dentistry

Gentle, friendly dental care for little smiles. We help children develop healthy habits that last a lifetime in a fun, welcoming environment.

Building Healthy Smiles from the Start

We welcome children from when their first teeth appear. Early visits to the dentist help children feel comfortable in the dental environment and establish good oral health habits that will benefit them throughout their lives.

Child-Friendly Treatment Room

Our Approach to Children's Dentistry

We understand that visiting the dentist can be daunting for children. Our team is specially trained to make dental visits fun and stress-free:

  • Gentle, patient approach with plenty of time for each child
  • Explanations in child-friendly language
  • "Tell-Show-Do" technique to reduce anxiety
  • Praise and encouragement throughout
  • Welcoming environment designed to put children at ease

Services for Children

  • Check-ups: Regular examinations to monitor development and catch problems early
  • Fissure Sealants: Protective coatings applied to back teeth to prevent decay
  • Fluoride Applications: Strengthen tooth enamel and prevent cavities
  • Fillings: Gentle treatment for cavities if needed
  • Oral Hygiene Education: Teaching proper brushing and flossing techniques
  • Dietary Advice: Guidance on foods and drinks that affect dental health

NHS Dental Care for Children is FREE

All NHS dental treatment is free for children under 18 (or under 19 if in full-time education). This includes check-ups, fillings, extractions, and preventive treatments like fissure sealants and fluoride applications.

When Should My Child First Visit the Dentist?

We recommend bringing your child for their first dental visit as soon as their first teeth appear, or by their first birthday. Early visits allow us to:

  • Check that teeth are developing normally
  • Provide advice on caring for baby teeth
  • Discuss feeding practices and their effect on teeth
  • Help your child feel comfortable with dental visits
Happy Child at Dentist

Tips for Parents

  • Start brushing as soon as the first tooth appears
  • Use a fluoride toothpaste appropriate for your child's age
  • Supervise brushing until age 7
  • Limit sugary snacks and drinks
  • Make dental visits a positive experience - never use the dentist as a threat
  • Lead by example with your own oral hygiene
