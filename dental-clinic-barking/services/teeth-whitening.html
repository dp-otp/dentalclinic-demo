Professional Teeth Whitening

Achieve a dramatically brighter smile with our professional teeth whitening treatments. Safe, effective, and supervised by dental professionals for the best results.

Brighten Your Smile

Professional teeth whitening is one of the most popular cosmetic dental treatments we offer at Abbey Dental Practice. Unlike over-the-counter whitening products, our treatments deliver dramatically better results in a safe, controlled environment.

Before & After Photo

Over time, teeth can become stained from coffee, tea, red wine, smoking, and certain foods. Our whitening treatments safely remove these stains to reveal the natural brightness of your teeth.

Our Whitening Options

We offer two professional whitening options to suit your lifestyle and preferences:

In-Office Whitening

Immediate results in just one appointment. Perfect for special occasions or quick transformations.

Take-Home Kits

Custom-made trays with professional-strength gel. Whiten at your convenience over 1-2 weeks.

Safe & Supervised

All treatments are supervised by qualified dental professionals to ensure safety.

Long-Lasting Results

Results can last up to 3 years with proper care and occasional touch-ups.

What to Expect

Before whitening, we'll examine your teeth to ensure you're a good candidate. We'll check for any cavities, gum disease, or existing restorations that might affect the treatment.

  • Initial consultation and shade assessment
  • Professional cleaning to remove surface stains
  • Application of whitening gel (in-office) or fitting of custom trays (take-home)
  • Results typically 2-8 shades whiter
  • Aftercare advice for maintaining your new bright smile
Treatment Process Photo

Is Teeth Whitening Right for You?

Professional teeth whitening works best on natural teeth with yellow or brown staining. Crowns, veneers, and fillings won't change colour with whitening treatments. During your consultation, we'll discuss realistic expectations and whether whitening is the best option for achieving your desired results.

Aftercare Tips

  • Avoid staining foods and drinks for 48 hours after treatment
  • Use a straw for tea, coffee, and dark beverages
  • Maintain good oral hygiene with regular brushing and flossing
  • Consider touch-up treatments every 6-12 months
  • Visit us for regular hygiene appointments
