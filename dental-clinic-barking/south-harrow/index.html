Our Services

Comprehensive dental care

From routine check-ups to cosmetic transformations, we provide the full range of dental services at our South Harrow practice.

General Dentistry

Routine check-ups, cleanings, fillings, and preventive care to keep your smile healthy.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Teeth whitening, porcelain veneers, and smile makeovers to help you feel confident.

Restorative Dentistry

Crowns, bridges, and dentures to restore damaged or missing teeth beautifully.

Root Canal Treatment

Advanced endodontic care to save infected teeth and relieve pain effectively.

Oral Surgery

Expert extractions and surgical procedures with gentle, experienced care.

Children's Dentistry

Gentle, patient care for young smiles in a friendly, welcoming environment.
