Practice Image
Our Story

Growing with London

Abbey Dental Practice has been providing dental care to London communities for over two decades. What started as a single practice has grown into a network of 4 locations serving Barking, Walthamstow, Whitechapel, and South Harrow.

Each of our modern practices has been designed with patient comfort and clinical excellence in mind, featuring state-of-the-art equipment and a welcoming environment.

"We aim to deliver a patient focused, quality dental service in a safe and friendly environment by staff committed to continuously developing their skills."

Our experienced team is committed to continuing the tradition of compassionate, comprehensive dental care that has made Abbey Dental Practice a trusted name across London.
