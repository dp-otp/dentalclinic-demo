Our Story

A practice built on trust and quality care

The Heathway Dental Surgery has been a cornerstone of the Dagenham community, providing exceptional dental services with a personal touch. Located at 276a Heathway, we're proud to serve our diverse, vibrant neighbourhood.

We believe in creating a welcoming environment where every patient feels comfortable and cared for. Our modern facility combines state-of-the-art technology with compassionate care.

We're committed to inclusivity and diversity, and we're proud to be a welcoming space where everyone is treated with dignity and respect.

Our Team

Expert care, personal touch

Our experienced team is dedicated to providing exceptional care in a comfortable, welcoming environment.

Dr Niti Kaushal

Principal Dentist

Our practice is led by Dr Niti Kaushal, an experienced dental professional dedicated to providing exceptional care. Our team is committed to continuous professional development, ensuring we stay at the forefront of modern dental techniques and technologies.

We're NHS registered and CQC quality approved, which means we meet the highest standards of care and safety. Our diverse team reflects the community we serve, and we're fluent in multiple languages to better communicate with all our patients.

From routine check-ups to complex procedures, every member of our team is committed to making your dental experience as comfortable and effective as possible.
